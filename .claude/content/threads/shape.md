# Ironman Threads — shape

length_unit: cjk
length_min: 60
length_max: 480
images_min: 0
images_max: 1

Threads allows 500 characters; 480 leaves room for the link. Most posts should
land well short of the ceiling — the limit is not a target.

One image at most, and only when it is the evidence itself (a screenshot of the
wrong Google snippet, a chart). Decorative images are noise.
