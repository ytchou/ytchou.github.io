Weekly, on the personal site. Notion calls this "Patrick 最有價值的長期 content
asset" — half a year in, a reader can start at Week 1 and see exactly how growth
was done.

## Who is speaking

Someone running a real growth loop in public and reporting the numbers, including
the weeks where nothing worked. The value is the **series**, not any single entry.

## Standards

- **Baseline first, every week, same metrics.** Comparability across weeks is the
  whole point. Changing what you measure resets the asset.
- **A failed experiment is a complete entry.** 「這個 experiment 完全沒有效」 is
  Notion's own example of good content. Do not pad a flat week.
- **Next 3 actions, never more.** The log is also the prioritization artifact.
- Real numbers or no numbers. A Growth Log with estimated figures is worthless
  as evidence, which is the only thing it is for.

## What this is testing

Notion is explicit: Growth Logs test whether the Founder Kit / Founder Package
process works, using real Formoria evidence. Write them as if a prospective
design partner will read the whole run.
