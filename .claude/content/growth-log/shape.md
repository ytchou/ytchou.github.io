## Fixed format (Notion §5B)

Same headings every week — comparability is the asset:

1. **Baseline** — weekly users, organic, social, referral, brand page views, outbound clicks
2. **This week's hypothesis**
3. **Experiments** — A, B, …
4. **Results**
5. **What worked**
6. **What failed**
7. **What I learned**
8. **Next 3 actions**

Cadence: one per week during the 30-day sprint and after.
