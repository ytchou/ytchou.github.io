# Evergreen synthesis — persona

Every one to two weeks, on the personal site.

## The rule that defines this line

> 不是把 7 篇 iThome 貼成一篇，而是重新整理成 timeless article。

A synthesis essay is **not** a digest. If it can be described as "days 11–16,
combined," it has failed. It makes an argument the daily articles could not,
because a daily article is bound to what happened that day.

The iThome series supplies evidence. The essay supplies the claim.

## Who is reading

Someone who did not read the series and never will. The essay has to stand alone
a year from now, when the specific numbers have moved.

## Notion's own examples

- 一人團隊開始做產品前，我現在會先回答的五個問題
- AI 如何幫我把數百個台灣品牌整理成可營運的資料系統
- 為什麼「有 Analytics」跟「知道該做什麼」是兩回事
- 我怎麼建立一個每週可重複的 Growth Review
- 社群曝光為什麼常常沒有變成網站流量

Each is a thesis, not a topic. That is the test.

## Standards

- One argument per essay, stated early enough that the reader can disagree.
- Evidence from Formoria, but the claim must survive the specifics changing.
- Timeless framing: no 「這 30 天」, no day numbers, no 「上週」.
