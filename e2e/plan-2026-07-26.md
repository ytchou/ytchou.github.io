## Journeys requiring coverage

### Browse the bilingual Resources hub and open either topic (P1)

- Actor: Public visitor.
- Goal: Discover the Resources hub from the site and open Design or AI security in the selected language.
- Pre-conditions: The statically generated site is available with the Resources collection.
- Steps: Open `/` or `/zh/`; follow Resources; verify both topic cards; open Design and AI security from the hub.
- Expected: English routes stay under `/resources`; Traditional Chinese routes stay under `/zh/resources`; the hub shows 37 Design resources and 4 AI security resources with the localized `resources` / `項資源` labels; each topic directory renders its matching localized heading and count.
- Risk if broken: A newly promoted topic is unreachable, empty, mislabeled, or crosses locales.
- DB/auth state: None; static content only.
- Target project: Smoke, cross-browser (target unavailable).
- Destination spec path: `e2e/smoke/resources-navigation.spec.ts` (deferred).

### Browse grouped Design and AI security catalogs (P2)

- Actor: Public visitor.
- Goal: Scan the grouped catalogs and open a curated source for deeper browsing.
- Pre-conditions: Open a Design or AI security directory in either locale.
- Steps: Review the non-empty group headings and counts; inspect a catalog card; activate its preview or title link.
- Expected: On first load (unfiltered, grid view) the Design directory shows 9 non-empty groups and 37 resources; the AI security directory shows the Agentic security audits group and 4 resources; every card exposes localized copy and a source link that opens in a new tab. In list view the card's screenshot link is hidden, but the title link, description, and tags remain.
- Risk if broken: Visitors cannot visually discover, count, or reach the curated sources.
- DB/auth state: None; static content only.
- Target project: Deep, Chromium (target unavailable).
- Destination spec path: `e2e/tests/resources-catalogs.spec.ts` (deferred).
