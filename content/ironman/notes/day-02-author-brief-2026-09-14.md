# Day 02 — author's own words (2026-09-14), evidence for the draft

Opening passage (author-approved, use near verbatim):

Formoria 最早的版本是一個分類清單：選家居、選文具、選咖啡器具，篩完給你一排品牌。做完之後我自己用，發現我腦子裡浮出來的句子從來不長那樣。是「露營要帶的杯子」，是「搬新家想找一盞不刺眼的燈」，是「想送一個台灣做的、不會太貴的伴手禮」。這些句子裡沒有一個詞出現在我的分類樹上。使用者講的是情境，資料庫裡存的是分類名稱，兩邊對不起來。

Author's argument (English, paraphrase into zh-TW):

- As said in Day 01, a lot of the time we are trying to discover something. Today we discover through AI chat models (ChatGPT) or search engines (Google). They mostly serve not the brand itself but the marketplace / e-commerce platform that carries the product.
- It is all about ranking: whichever brand or platform has the highest bids or the best SEO. Search the same phrase over time and you get similar products. Vicious cycle: those products get impressions → more people buy → more marketing budget → more ads / SEO / team → more impressions. Brands with resources get noticed by the general public.
- What I want: a platform that searches for the product or service you actually need. Not a search engine optimizing click-through or revenue, but one that optimizes relevance.
- Example prompt: "I want a birthday gift for my best friend who I enjoy hiking with, who recently complains there is no durable product for hiking, and who likes darker colors more than vibrant colors." If the engine is tailored to the actual need, you get what you need instead of what has the most resources.
- The one-liner: instead of maximizing revenue, maximize relevance, by translating what people type into what they want. That means understanding the search intent behind the query. It also means extracting the information and functionality of every product and brand in the database, so we know which product fits the narrative.
- Day 02 lays the foundation for the work that follows. Questions to list, not answer: how to define features so they are extractable; how to extract them; how to define the taxonomy; which dimensions to encode as features; how the product should look.
- Product shape (high level): a search bar; a discovery layer where you find products not through search but other interfaces; a result page with many products and brands; click a brand → brand page with how to find them (social media, website) and which products they sell.
- Purpose: business context and foundation, mapped to the next days where the hard work (data science, AI engineering) happens.

Ownership: taxonomy = Day 03, wireframing = Day 04, data thesis = Day 05. Preview, do not demonstrate.
